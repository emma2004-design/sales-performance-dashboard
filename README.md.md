# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, costs, and profitability across different business dimensions.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales data, helping users explore business performance by region, product category, sales representative, sales channel, and customer segment.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue, costs, profit, and profit margin.
- Compare sales performance across regions and product categories.
- Evaluate monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare the performance of sales representatives.
- Understand customer segment and sales channel contributions.
- Present business information through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables and formulas
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive slicers and date timeline
- CSV for dataset exchange

## Dataset Description

The project uses a synthetic retail sales dataset containing 10,000 records covering the period from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025.

The dataset includes fields such as order date, county, region, customer segment, product category, sales representative, sales channel, payment method, quantity, unit price, cost, revenue, profit, and profit margin.

**Data note:** The dataset is synthetic and was created for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes. It does not represent verified transactions from a real company.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for total revenue, total profit, total cost, and profit margin.
- Revenue and profit trends by month.
- Sales comparisons by region and product category.
- Sales representative performance analysis.
- Sales channel and customer segment analysis.
- Interactive filters for region, sales representative, sales channel, category, customer segment, and order date.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard displays the following results for the dataset:

- Total Revenue: KES 632,286,142.50
- Total Profit: KES 149,687,444.40
- Total Cost: KES 482,598,698.10

These figures describe the synthetic dataset and should not be interpreted as the financial performance of a real business.

## Business Questions Addressed

1. What are the overall revenue, cost, and profit figures?
2. How does revenue change from month to month?
3. Which regions and product categories contribute to sales?
4. How do sales representatives compare?
5. Which sales channels and customer segments generate revenue?
6. How does profitability vary across the available business dimensions?

## Business Value

The dashboard demonstrates how raw sales records can be organized into meaningful performance indicators and interactive visualizations. It can help users explore trends, compare business segments, and identify areas that deserve further investigation.

## Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and may not reflect real market conditions.
- Business recommendations must be validated against real operational data before being used for actual business decisions.
- Findings depend on the accuracy of the dataset and the calculations used in the workbook.
